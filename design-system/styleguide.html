Layout primitives

  • .section — full-bleed block with fluid vertical + inline padding.
  • .band — shorter colored header strip (pair with a .bg-* tint).
  • .container — centers content at --container (940px).
  • .stack — vertical rhythm between children; .cluster — wrapping row.
  • .grid — equal columns via --cols / --cols-sm.
  • [data-reveal] — fades/slides in on scroll (each section here uses it).
